KEEP THE COST REALISTIC

Software first. Specialised hardware only where it earns its cost.

DEFAULT FOR MOST ORGANISATIONS

Post-quantum cryptography in existing systems

  • Upgrade TLS/VPN/API gateways, applications, and cloud services as they reach their normal maintenance cycle.
  • Use a central crypto policy so teams and suppliers follow the same approved standards.
  • Run hybrid classical + post-quantum key establishment during the transition when supported.
  • Reuse current networks and most current servers; budget mainly for inventory, testing, engineering, and training.

Broad protection with the lowest incremental infrastructure cost.

SELECTIVE HIGH-SECURITY LINKS

Quantum key distribution (QKD)

  • Use only for narrowly defined, extremely sensitive links where dedicated optical hardware and operational overhead are justified.
  • QKD distributes keys; it still needs authenticated endpoints and conventional encryption for the actual data.
  • It is not the low-cost, universal replacement for post-quantum cryptography.

A specialised complement—not the baseline rollout plan.

A MANAGEABLE ROLLOUT

How a large-scale transition starts.

  1. 0–3 months

    Inventory & ownership

    Assign an accountable team, map high-value cryptography, and identify systems that cannot be upgraded easily.

  2. 3–9 months

    Pilot & test

    Upgrade one low-risk but meaningful path, validate performance, and test interoperability with suppliers.

  3. 9–24 months

    Scale by risk

    Prioritise exposed and long-lived data systems; bake post-quantum requirements into procurement and refresh cycles.

  4. Continuous

    Operate crypto-agile

    Keep an inventory, rotate capabilities through policy, and remain ready as standards and risk change.
